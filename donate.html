Where your money goes

What your gift makes possible

GHS 50

Provides flashcards and a workbook for one child — and contributes to Foundation Support: school materials, furniture, or clothing for a child in need.

GHS 150

Funds one full classroom cognitive development kit — puzzles, flashcards, and activity books for 30 pupils.

GHS 500

Helps bring cognitive development resources to an entire small primary school.

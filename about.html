About Anyoka Foundation

A name that means to catch — and a mission to match.

We're a Ghanaian non-governmental organisation working to make sure no child's potential goes unclaimed.

Anyoka means "to catch."

Our mission is to help children catch opportunities, catch knowledge, catch confidence, and catch their full potential.

Our founding story

"Why do young people in places like China develop the capacity to innovate so early in life?"

That question, asked in 2025, became the starting point for everything we now build.

How we began

The question that started it all

Anyoka Foundation was born out of a divine assignment — a calling to ask the questions many overlook. Why do young people in other parts of the world, especially in countries like China, develop the capacity to create, invent, and innovate at such an early age? What are they being taught that unlocks that potential?

The answer became clear: it begins with cognitive development. Many of us were never given the tools to fully engage our minds — to think critically, solve problems creatively, or develop our innate intelligence — simply because we were not exposed to that kind of learning growing up. Without those tools, even the best opportunities pass us by unclaimed.

Anyoka Foundation exists to make sure every Ghanaian child is equipped to catch what life offers them, and one day, to build the kind of innovations the world looks to Ghana for.

Anyoka means "to catch" — a meaning that speaks directly to the mission: helping children catch opportunities, catch knowledge, catch confidence, and catch their full potential.

What guides us

Vision, mission, and values

🌍

Vision

A generation of Ghanaians who are cognitively empowered from birth — capable of catching every opportunity placed in front of them, and going on to innovate, create, and lead on the world stage.

🎯

Mission

To help children catch opportunities, catch knowledge, catch confidence, and catch their full potential — through brain-based learning programmes, games, and environments for ages 1–24.

🤝

Foundation Support

Alongside our cognitive programmes, we address the conditions that make learning possible — school furniture, clothing access, learning materials, and basic health screening. We do not separate the mind from what it needs to thrive.

⭐

Values

  • Divine purpose and intentionality
  • Every child has unlimited potential to catch
  • Brain-first, evidence-based learning
  • Equity for every child
  • Innovation rooted in African identity

Leadership

Our leadership team

Justice Ayoka

Founder & Executive Director

Justice Ayoka is an IT consultant and entrepreneur based in Greater Accra, Ghana. Growing up, he had early access to cognitive games that he believes shaped a mathematical ability he couldn’t always fully explain — and that experience made him ask why every Ghanaian child shouldn’t have the same advantage. Anyoka Foundation is his answer to that question.

Emma Afenoko

Non-Executive Director

Emma Afenoko is a banker and Non-Executive Director of Anyoka Foundation. An advocate for unlocking creative potential, Emma holds a deep conviction that no one should be denied the chance to develop their cognitive abilities for lack of support — a belief rooted in watching talented friends whose gifts went unrealised while growing up. Together with her husband, she makes every effort to give their children the opportunity to develop their full potential. She brings that memory, and the determination it created, to everything she does at Anyoka.

Razak Sulleyman

Non-Executive Director

Razak Sulleyman is a mining professional and Non-Executive Director of Anyoka Foundation. A family man with a deep personal commitment to children’s education, Razak joined the board out of a conviction that every child deserves the opportunity to develop their full potential. He brings practical grounding and a genuine belief in the cause to Anyoka’s leadership.

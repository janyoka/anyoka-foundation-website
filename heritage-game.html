Ghana Heritage Game

Explore Ghana's history and culture through photographs, quizzes, and a memory match challenge. Learn about the places, people, and traditions that make Ghana extraordinary.

Ghana Heritage Sites

Explore Ghana's most significant historical and cultural landmarks — from the slave trade routes of the north to the independence monuments of Accra, and the living traditions that continue today.

Who Am I? Where Am I?

Read the clue. Think carefully. Then click the card to reveal the answer and the photograph. Can you get all nine?

How to play Read each clue carefully — it describes a real place or person from Ghanaian history and heritage. Click the card to reveal the answer. Challenge a friend to see who gets more right without looking.

Memory Match

Find the matching pairs. Eight heritage sites, sixteen tiles — flip two at a time and try to remember where each image is hiding.

Moves: 0 · Matches: 0 / 8
Tip for teachers Project the memory match on a board and let students take turns calling out which tiles to flip. A great 10-minute warm-up activity that builds visual memory and cultural knowledge at the same time.
🎉
This game is completely free — forever. Ghana's history and heritage belongs to every Ghanaian child. Share it with a friend, a classmate, or a school. No login needed. No data collected.

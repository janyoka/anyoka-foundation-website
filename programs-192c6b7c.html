Programme 04

Foundation Support

Anyoka Foundation recognises that cognitive development cannot happen without certain basic conditions being met. Where we work with schools and communities, we address the physical and environmental barriers that stop learning before it starts — not as charity for its own sake, but as the foundation our cognitive programmes are built on.

School furniture & supplies

Desks, chairs, and learning materials provided to schools where children are sitting on floors or sharing one book between many.

Clothing support

Where lack of school clothing keeps children from attending, we address that barrier directly so they can show up and learn.

Basic health access

Health screening and referrals at our programme sites — ensuring every person we work with is physically ready to engage with their own development.

Foundation Support is available across all ages we serve — 1 to 24. It is always in service of one goal: ensuring every person is ready, equipped, and able to fully catch what is being offered to them.

Programme 05 · Phase 1 Pilot

Anyoka Play

A free, browser-based learning platform aligned with the Ghana GES/NaCCA curriculum — built by Anyoka Learning for children in Basic 3 to Basic 6. Mathematics is live now. English, Science, and more are coming.

GES/NaCCA curriculum-aligned

Every activity maps directly to the primary mathematics syllabus — reinforcing exactly what teachers are already teaching.

Any device, anywhere

Works in any browser on any phone. No app download, no IT setup, no passwords for children.

Built in Ghana, for Ghana

Questions set in Ghanaian contexts — Makola market, Kejetia, community life children actually know.

School Donations

Give a child a brain boost.

We place cognitive development kits — flashcards, puzzles, and activity books — directly into under-resourced classrooms across Ghana. Alongside kit donations, our Foundation Support programme provides school furniture, clothing assistance, and basic health screening where these are barriers to learning.

Phase 1 Pilot

A Mathematics Tool Built Around the GES Curriculum

Anyoka Play is a browser-based mathematics practice platform built by Anyoka Learning and aligned with the Ghana GES/NaCCA curriculum. Children play short, engaging sessions that reinforce what you are already teaching — set in familiar Ghanaian contexts like Makola market, Kejetia, and community life they recognise.

This is Phase 1. The platform is new, content is growing weekly, and your feedback shapes everything that comes next. We are grateful your class is among the very first.

Free in Phase 1. Anyoka Play is completely free during this pilot. As the platform grows and expands to more subjects, a small, affordable access fee may be introduced — but our commitment is that cost will never be a barrier for Ghanaian children and schools.

How to Get Your Class Started

Phase 1 account setup: During this pilot, the Anyoka Learning team creates accounts for teachers and registers children. You do not need to handle any technical setup. Simply send us your class list and we set everything up within 24 hours.
1
Email your class list — full names of each child as you want them registered — to info@anyokafoundation.org
2
Receive your school code and confirmation within 24 hours
3
Share the school code with your class and direct children to anyokaplay.anyokafoundation.org
4
Children type their name — no passwords, no email required — and start playing immediately
